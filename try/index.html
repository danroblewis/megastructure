Identity experiments

What makes a cell part of you. Each is the whole game with one rule replaced. The baseline is the main game. Numbers and verdicts are in ROADMAP.md.

  • coherence — cells belong by agreement of motion habit
  • familiarity — cells belong by how long their neighbourhood has stayed stable
  • commitment — cells follow the intention they have committed to; Marcia's four statuses
  • phase — each cell keeps a rhythm; one self is one coherent beat (shared state survives parting, but no lasting boundary)
  • history — cells belong by what they have lived through (heat, cold, current, ore); a wisp is a shared past. Overlaps without passing through, learns together
  • agreement — cells hold a stance and average it only with neighbours who roughly agree; attention nudges those who already agree with you
  • statuses — Marcia reframed: no commitment to intentions; the whole wisp is in moratorium while it changes, reaches achievement by holding any shape, and falls into negative identity when it stabilises on what harms it
  • return — identity is what comes back: an excursion that returns is kept and strengthened, a cell that never returns fades; returned cells glow gold
  • held — the boundary as an act of will: a second finger (or space) firms your edge and refuses exchange across it, at a cost in stamina
  • survival — no new physics; a ledger at each cold room of what came through and what was lost
